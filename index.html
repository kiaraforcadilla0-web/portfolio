About Me

I am a detail-oriented and dedicated QA Engineer with hands-on experience in Manual Testing, Test Automation, Test Case Development and Execution, Regression Testing, Sanity Testing, and Defect Management.

I have practical knowledge of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC), as well as experience working in an Agile and Scrum environment.

My experience includes identifying and documenting software defects, creating clear and detailed issue tickets, investigating failed test executions, analyzing root causes, and collaborating with developers and team members to ensure software quality.

I also have hands-on experience creating and executing automated test scripts using AHQ's No-Code Automation Platform, monitoring test executions, and assisting with Staging Environment deployments using GitHub.

Aside from testing activities, I have experience conducting website and application demonstrations, presenting system functionalities, and facilitating QA learning sessions and peer knowledge-sharing activities to help team members understand software testing concepts and best practices.

In addition to my QA background, I have experience as a Virtual Assistant and Student Assistant, where I developed skills in administrative support, email management, data entry, organization, communication, and multitasking.

I also have a technical background in web development, with knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL. I independently developed and tested an Event Attendance System as my academic thesis project.

I am passionate about continuous learning, problem-solving, teamwork, and delivering reliable, efficient, and high-quality software applications.
